Save Today, Use Tomorrow

A Castletroy community campaign for a sustainable future

From rising seas to everyday energy choices, the climate is constantly changing. But Castletroy is already proving that local action can be part of the solution.

A Warmer World

Burning fossil fuels releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, trapping heat and causing global temperatures to rise. This leads to more extreme weather events like heatwaves, droughts, and floods, which harm our agriculture, water supplies and wildlife by shifting seasons, melting ice and worsening droughts and floods, and destroying habitats. This extra heat culminates, putting strain on the very systems people and nature depend on.

The Rising Seas

As global temperatures rise, polar ice caps and glaciers melt, adding more water to the oceans, while seawater itself expands as it warms, raising sea levels and worsening storm surges, flooding and erosion that threaten coastal communities, vital habitats such as mangroves and wetlands, and infrastructure, like roads, ports and water systems. Ireland as an island nation is particularly vulnerable to these changes.

A witness to Change

“We live our comfortable lives in the shadow of a disaster of our own making. That disaster is being brought about by the very things that allow us to live our comfortable lives.”— Sir David Attenborough, A Life on Our Planet (2020)

Attenborough's witness statement reminds us that the world is our lifeblood. 'Save Today, Use Tomorrow' turns that global messaage into local choices in Castletroy.
