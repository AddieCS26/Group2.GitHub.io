Source: UNFCCC

The Impact Of Environmental Changes On Our Planet

❋ Nature Loss

Deforestation has had drastic, long-lasting impacts on our planet: From the decline of tropical habitats to the spread of infectious diseases such as Ebola and Lassa Fever, the effects of deforestation are widespread. Beyond the loss of natural resources, deforestation turns productive forest ecosystems into degraded landscapes, reducing their ability to support economic growth and sustainable development.

≋ Rising Sea Levels

As water warms, it expands. Sea temperatures are rising faster than usual, which is a major concern. The warming of the planet causes glaciers and ice caps to melt, leading to rising sea levels. Over time, this can result in increased flooding, damage to coral reefs, and the degradation of farmland. These impacts can reduce food production and cause harmful substances, such as pesticides, to wash into the ocean, further damaging marine ecosystems.

◈ Pollution

Pollution is one of the most serious environmental challenges facing the world today. Air pollution, plastic waste and chemical contamination harm ecosystems, threaten wildlife and endanger human health. Millions of tonnes of plastic enter the oceans each year, damaging marine life and disrupting food chains. Reducing pollution through sustainable practices and responsible waste management is essential for protecting the environment and ensuring a healthier future for generations to come.
