Why Each Pledge Matters

The four pledge actions and why they matter
Pledge Why it matters
Reduce plastic Single-use plastics like bottles, bags, and straws often end up in oceans where they harm marine life. By choosing reusable alternatives, you help cut down the millions of tons of plastic waste produced every year.
Save energy Turning off lights, unplugging devices, and using energy-efficient appliances lowers electricity demand. This reduces greenhouse gas emissions from power plants and helps slow climate change.
Recycle more Recycling paper, plastic, glass, and metal keeps materials in use instead of sending them to landfill. It also reduces the need to extract new raw materials, saving natural resources and energy.
Use sustainable transport Walking, cycling, carpooling, or taking public transport instead of driving alone reduces fuel consumption and air pollution. Fewer cars on the road also mean less traffic and cleaner cities.
